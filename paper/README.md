# The research paper

**[Fin-ally Supported: Shark-fin supports for long, flat, tilted overhangs](paper.md)**, version 1.0, first published
19 October 2026, by Matthew Armstrong · [PDF](fin-ally-supported-v1.0.pdf)

Everything behind Shark Support written up: the problem, prior art, the method in enough detail to rebuild it, the
mistakes, the numbers, a test plan, and a defensive disclosure of the method and its variations, dedicated to the
public. Nothing in it has been printed yet: numbers come from the code, slicer estimates and G-code.

Published on its own at [The-Dorkknight/fin-ally-supported](https://github.com/The-Dorkknight/fin-ally-supported),
with the tools to redraw the figures and rebuild the PDF; this folder is a copy that travels with the plugin.
